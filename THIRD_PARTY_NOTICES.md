# Third-party notices

## Cape Town Physio staff handbook (the `handbook/` folder)

The demo Library markdown in `handbook/` is **original sample content** for the Cape Town Physio product suite (Atlas, Counter, Clerk). It is fictional practice policy text for demos and is **not** real HR or clinical guidance for any live clinic.

## Legacy TTS Handbook build script

`scripts/build_handbook.py` can still rebuild the old US GSA TTS Handbook pages from <https://github.com/18F/handbook> (public domain / CC0). That upstream content is **not** what ships in `handbook/` today; the physio pages are maintained directly in this repo.
