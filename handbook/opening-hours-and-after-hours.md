# Opening hours and after-hours messages

## Clinic hours

We are open **Monday to Friday**. We **close at 5:00 PM** on weekdays.

We are **closed on Saturday and Sunday** and on public holidays.

## After-hours contact

Patients who email or message outside hours receive the agreed auto-reply: **"We got your message. We will reply on the next working day."**

Staff do not need to send that sentence manually when Clerk is enabled — it is pre-approved.

Do not confirm appointment times after hours unless a manager has approved the booking in Clerk.
