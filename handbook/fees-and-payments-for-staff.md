# Fees and payments (staff reference)

Patient-facing fees match the website:

- Standard one-on-one session: **R650**
- Initial assessment (45 minutes): **R650**

Payment is due at the end of each visit. We accept **card** and **EFT**.

We do not publish medical-aid tariff codes on the site. Reception handles medical-aid questions by phone.

Staff must not invent prices in email or chat. If the fee is not on the published page, say you will confirm and escalate.
