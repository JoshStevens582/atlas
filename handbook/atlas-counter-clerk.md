# Atlas, Counter, and Clerk — for staff

## Atlas (this app)

Staff ask Atlas about **this handbook**. Atlas shows the page it used. If the handbook does not say it, Atlas says it does not know.

## Counter

Public chat on the website. Answers **patients** from fees, hours, and services. Does not book or send mail.

## Clerk

Does paperwork after a patient or staff question was answered. Sends pre-agreed short replies automatically. Bookings and outbound email wait for a manager **yes**.

## Same practice

All three read approved pages for **Cape Town Physio**. Staff questions → Atlas. Patient questions on the site → Counter. Tasks after that → Clerk.
