# Reception and bookings

## Booking rules

Bookings are taken during opening hours only.

Standard sessions are **R650** — see the patient fees page. Quote only what is on the published fees page.

Do **not** hold a diary slot as confirmed until the owner or reception lead has **approved** the booking in Clerk. Until then, tell the patient the request is **received**, not confirmed.

## Phone and desk

Answer within **three rings** when possible. Take a callback number for every enquiry.

Medical-aid tariff codes are **not** published on the website. Tell callers to phone reception for medical-aid queries.

## When Counter and Clerk are in use

**Counter** answers patient questions from the website (fees, hours, services). Counter does not book.

**Clerk** drafts bookings and patient emails after Counter or reception has answered. Clerk waits for a **yes** before anything goes out.
