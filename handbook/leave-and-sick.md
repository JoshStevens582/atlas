# Leave and sick leave

## Annual leave

Full-time staff receive **15 working days** of annual leave per leave cycle.

Staff may carry over up to **5 days** of unused annual leave into the next cycle. Carry-over days must be taken within the first **three months** of the new cycle unless the practice manager agrees in writing.

Email **HR** with your manager copied to request carry-over or to book leave.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is for illness or medical appointments. Notify your manager **before 8:00 AM** on the first day you are absent, or as soon as reasonably possible.

You may self-certify up to **2 consecutive days** once per quarter without a doctor's note. Longer absences require a medical certificate.

## Unpaid leave

Unpaid leave may be granted at the manager's discretion when annual leave is exhausted. It is not automatic.
