# Patient communication

## Tone

Be clear, calm, and professional. We treat adults; we are not an emergency service.

## What we say on the website

Patients see fees, hours, and services on the public site. **Counter** answers from those pages only.

If Counter does not know, staff should not guess — check the page or ask a senior therapist.

## Email and messages

Marketing promises or clinical advice beyond the handbook are **not** allowed in outbound mail.

Use Clerk to draft patient emails from approved pages. A manager approves before send.
